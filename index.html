PeriDoc: HWP PDF Office Viewer

Open HWP, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files offline. Fast and private.

Google Play

PeriDoc is a fast, free document viewer for Android. Open HWP, HWPX, PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX) and PowerPoint (PPTX) files right on your phone or tablet. No account, no upload.

HWP and HWPX viewer

Got a .hwp file from a Korean school, company or government office? PeriDoc opens Hangul word processor documents (HWP, HWPX, HWP 3.0, HML) made with Hancom Office, keeping tables, images and page layout. Korean fonts are built in, so text looks right even on phones without them.

PDF reader

Read PDF files smoothly with search, text selection, table of contents, page thumbnails and password-protected PDF support.

Office document viewer

Read Word documents (DOCX), Excel spreadsheets (XLSX) and PowerPoint presentations (PPTX) without installing a full office suite.

More file types

Text (TXT), Markdown (MD), CSV, HTML and images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP).

Features

  • Single page or continuous scrolling, two pages side by side in landscape, pinch to zoom
  • Search inside documents, select and copy text, copy tables as CSV
  • Jump around with the table of contents and page thumbnails
  • Picks up where you stopped reading
  • Rotate pages, save a page as an image, save as PDF, share or open in another app
  • Built-in file manager that finds every document on your device, with Trash and home screen shortcuts
  • Set PeriDoc as your default app and open attachments from email, messengers, Downloads and file managers with one tap
  • Import your own fonts from a PC

Private and offline

  • Your documents stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the screens that draw documents are blocked from the network.
  • Works offline. No sign-up, no login.
  • The internet is used only for a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen (Google AdMob).

App languages: English and Korean.
