PeriDoc: HWP PDF Office Viewer

Open HWP files, PDFs and Office docs offline. Fast, light and private.

Google Play

PeriDoc is a fast, free and secure document viewer for Android. Open HWP files, HWPX, PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX) and PowerPoint (PPTX) directly on your phone or tablet. No account required, and your files are never uploaded.

HWP and HWPX viewer

Need to open HWP files from a Korean school, company or government office? PeriDoc opens Hangul word processor documents (HWP, HWPX, HWP 3.0, HML) created with Hancom Office, preserving tables, images and page layouts. Korean fonts are built in so text looks right, and you can import your own PC fonts for an even closer match.

PDF reader

Read PDF files smoothly with support for text search, selection and copying, table of contents, page thumbnails, and password-protected PDFs.

Office document viewer

Easily view Word documents (DOCX), Excel spreadsheets (XLSX) and PowerPoint presentations (PPTX) without installing heavy office suites.

Search inside your documents (with OCR)

Find text inside your documents, not just file names. PeriDoc indexes the files on your device so a search instantly shows which file and page contains the match. Text in scanned PDFs and photos is recognized strictly on-device (OCR) and included in your results.

Smart search (optional)

Can't recall the exact words? Smart search understands context and meaning to find relevant documents and scenes in your photos. Download the model once (about 388 MB), and from then on all processing stays on your device, without the internet.

Deadline cards

Open a notice or announcement and PeriDoc finds labeled dates such as application periods, deadlines and event times, then shows them above the document with a countdown. Tap to add one to your calendar or call the contact number. Works with dates in Korean and English documents.

Search by image (with smart search)

Use one image to find similar images, or take a photo of a printed page to find the original file on your device.

Version comparison and duplicate cleanup

PeriDoc groups versions of the same document, like "report_final" and "report_v2", and highlights what was changed, added or deleted between two versions right on the page. On a wide screen, see both versions side by side. Find identical files and move the extra copies to the Trash.

Optimized for foldables and tablets

Hold a Galaxy Tab or an unfolded Galaxy Fold sideways to see your file list and document side by side. In Flex mode (half-folded), the document stays on the top half with page controls conveniently on the bottom. Keyboard shortcuts (like Ctrl+F) and mouse wheel scrolling are supported.

More supported file types

Text (TXT), Markdown (MD), CSV, HTML and images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP).

Features

  • Single page or continuous scrolling, two pages side by side in landscape, pinch to zoom
  • Search inside documents, select and copy text, copy tables as CSV
  • Navigate easily using the table of contents and page thumbnails, and resume reading where you left off
  • Recent searches, view all matches in a document at a glance, and use the app icon shortcut for quick searches
  • Rotate pages, save a page as an image, save as PDF, share, or open in another app
  • Built-in file manager to locate every document on your device, complete with a Trash folder and home screen shortcuts
  • Set as your default app to open attachments instantly from Gmail, messengers, Downloads and file managers
  • Built-in Korean fonts and the ability to import custom PC fonts

Private and offline

  • PeriDoc never uploads your documents or sends them anywhere, and the view that draws your documents has no network access.
  • Indexing, text recognition (OCR), smart search, deadline cards and version comparison all run entirely on your device without sending data anywhere.
  • Opens documents offline. No sign-up, no login required.
  • The internet is used only for a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen (Google AdMob) and for downloading the smart search model.
